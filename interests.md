# Gaming

WANT:
- New game announcements and release dates
- Major updates, expansions or DLC for games I play: (add your games here, e.g. Final Fantasy XIV, Baldur's Gate 3, Elden Ring)
- JRPGs and CRPGs
- Japanese releases and Japan-only news
- Industry news with real consequences: layoffs, studio closures, acquisitions, major leadership changes
- Big hardware news (new consoles, handhelds, price changes)

SKIP:
- Console wars and platform-loyalty arguments
- Sales-chart arguments and "is X a flop" threads
- Review-score drama, Metacritic/OpenCritic threads
- "What are you playing" and other recurring community threads
- Memes, polls, "unpopular opinion" threads
- Rumors and leaks without a credible, named source
- Deals and sales threads

# Good news

WANT:
- Measurable progress in health and medicine (approved treatments, large trials, falling disease rates)
- Science results that are replicated or large-scale
- Climate, energy and conservation outcomes with numbers (emissions, species recovery, protected areas)

- Poverty, development and education progress backed by data

SKIP:
- Celebrity charity and feel-good human-interest stories
- Animal-rescue virals
- "One weird trick" science hype
